The view DSL now ships a `property` filter — `(property . (("PROP" . "VALUE") ...))`, AND-combined, implemented in org-gtd-view-language.el (org-gtd-5, 2026-07-23). The interactive view builder must expose it too: the "Implement tertiary general org-mode filters" subtask should let users add arbitrary property (PROP . VALUE) pairs to a view, since hand-written specs already support it. Keep the builder and the DSL in lockstep — the DSL knows `not-habit` and `property`; the builder should surface both.
