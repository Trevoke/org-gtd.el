* How to capture and process the inbox
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: capture-and-process
:END:

This is the daily routine: get items out of your head into the inbox,
then walk the inbox to zero by clarifying and organizing each one.
The recipes below assume the keybindings from
[[#install-and-configure][How to install and configure]].

** Capture from anywhere

Run =M-x org-gtd-capture= (default binding =C-c d c=). It opens an
=org-capture= buffer with two templates:

- =i= — Inbox. The default. Plain heading, then notes.
- =l= — Inbox with link. Same, plus a link back to wherever you were
  when you triggered capture.

Type a short heading. Don't try to make it perfect — capture is for
emptying your head, not for deciding. Press =C-c C-c= to save and
close, or =C-c C-k= to abandon.

Captured items get an =ORG_GTD_CAPTURED_AT= timestamp automatically.

** Capture from the agenda

When the cursor is on a task in =org-agenda= and you decide it
belongs in the inbox instead, you can re-capture it. The marker at
beginning-of-line is =org-get-at-bol 'org-marker= — most users do not
need to call this directly; running =M-x org-gtd-capture= from the
agenda still files into the inbox normally.

If you instead want to *re-clarify* an existing org heading (whether
in the agenda, a project file, or anywhere else), put your cursor on
it and run =M-x org-gtd-clarify-item=. That opens the same WIP
clarify buffer used for inbox items, without going through capture.

** Process the inbox

Run =M-x org-gtd-process-inbox= (default binding =C-c d p=). It walks
each heading in =inbox.org= one at a time. After the main inbox is
empty, processing continues into anything you listed in
=org-gtd-additional-inbox-files=.

For each item, a clarify buffer (in =org-gtd-clarify-mode=) opens.
This is your decision point.

** What clarify gives you

The clarify buffer is a temp-file-backed copy of the heading. You
can edit it freely — title, notes, sub-headings. Nothing you do here
touches your real GTD files until you finish.

Common edits during clarify:

- Rewrite the title to be specific and actionable
  ("Email Alex about Q3 budget" beats "Alex").
- Add notes you want to keep with the item.
- For projects, add tasks as second-level headings ~**~.

When you are happy with the wording, organize it: =C-c c= (or
=M-x org-gtd-organize=). A transient menu appears.

** Decide on a type

The transient lays out GTD's classification choices. Pick by what
the item actually is:

- =q= — *Quick action*. You can do it in under two minutes. Just do
  it now; org-gtd will mark it DONE and archive.
- =s= — *Next action*. A single concrete task you'll do when you
  can. Most one-off to-dos land here.
- =c= — *Calendar*. The task is tied to a specific time. Prompts for
  a timestamp.
- =d= — *Delegate*. Someone else owns it. Prompts for who, and when
  you want to follow up.
- =h= — *Habit*. A recurring action with a repeating schedule.
- =p= — *Project (multi-step)*. The current heading becomes the
  project; child headings become its tasks.
- =a= — *Add to existing project*. Treat the current item as a task
  belonging to a project you already have.
- =i= — *Tickler*. Bring it back on a specific date.
- =y= — *Someday/Maybe*. No date, no commitment yet.
- =k= — *Knowledge*. Reference material; archive into your
  knowledge area.
- =t= — *Trash*. Cancel and archive.

After you pick, =org-gtd-organize-hooks= run (by default that
prompts for tags), then the disposition fires (refile, archive, or
update in place). The next inbox item appears.

For the complete list of types and their fields, see
[[#type-registry][Reference: type registry]]. For the underlying
mechanics, see [[#about-the-pipeline][Explanation: about the pipeline]].

** When one item is actually several

You captured "Plan birthday party" but in clarify you realize it is
really five separate things. You have two options:

1. *Make it a project.* Add child headings under the captured
   heading — one task per ~**~ — then pick =p= (project) in the
   transient.

2. *Split it into independent items.* In the clarify buffer, run
   =M-x org-gtd-clarify-duplicate= to spawn a queued duplicate with
   a new title. Each duplicate becomes its own item to organize. Use
   =org-gtd-clarify-duplicate-exact= if you want a verbatim copy
   without retitling.

Splitting is the right call when the items belong to different
contexts ("Buy gift" is a next-action, "Reserve venue" is a project
of its own).

** Skip and stop

- =M-x org-gtd-clarify-stop= — abandon the current item without
  organizing. If there are more items in the inbox, processing
  continues with the next one.
- The default disposition for organize is to refile out of the
  inbox. If you ran clarify with =C-u= (or toggled "Update in place"
  in the transient), the heading is rewritten where it sits instead
  of being refiled.

** Verify by engaging

After processing, run =M-x org-gtd-engage= (=C-c d e=). You should
see your NEXT actions and any calendar items in the agenda. Items
filed as someday/tickler do not show here — they only return when
their date arrives or you reflect on them.
