* How to install and configure org-gtd
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: install-and-configure
:END:

You have Emacs and you want a working GTD setup. This guide gets
=org-gtd= loaded, configured, and verified end-to-end.

If you are coming from version 3.x and need to migrate existing data,
follow the upgrade chapter in the manual: [[info:org-gtd#upgrading]].

** Install from MELPA

org-gtd is published on MELPA. Make sure your =package-archives= include
MELPA, then:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
M-x package-install RET org-gtd RET
#+end_src

You also need =org-edna= installed and active — it is what makes
project task dependencies work. =package-install= pulls it in as a
dependency; you still have to enable it (~(org-edna-mode 1)~).

** Apply the minimum required configuration

Three settings are required for org-gtd to work:

1. =org-todo-keywords= — your TODO sequence, with all GTD keywords in
   the *same* sequence.
2. =org-gtd-keyword-mapping= — which of those keywords means
   "next-action" vs "waiting" vs "done", etc.
3. =org-edna-mode= — turned on, so projects advance correctly.

Two more settings are strongly recommended:

- =org-agenda-files= — include =org-gtd-directory= so engage and
  reflect commands have something to scan.
- =org-gtd-update-ack= — set to the major version you installed, so
  the upgrade banner stays quiet.

** Recipe: vanilla =use-package=

Drop this in your init file:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package org-gtd
  :ensure t
  :after org
  :demand t
  :init
  (setq org-gtd-update-ack "4.0.0")
  ;; (setq org-gtd-directory "~/gtd/")  ; default is ~/gtd/
  :custom
  (org-todo-keywords '((sequence "TODO" "NEXT" "WAIT" "|" "DONE" "CNCL")))
  (org-gtd-keyword-mapping '((todo . "TODO")
                             (next . "NEXT")
                             (wait . "WAIT")
                             (done . "DONE")
                             (canceled . "CNCL")))
  :config
  (org-edna-mode 1)
  (setq org-agenda-files (list org-gtd-directory))
  :bind
  (("C-c d c" . org-gtd-capture)
   ("C-c d e" . org-gtd-engage)
   ("C-c d p" . org-gtd-process-inbox)
   :map org-gtd-clarify-mode-map
   ("C-c c" . org-gtd-organize)
   :map org-agenda-mode-map
   ("C-c ." . org-gtd-agenda-transient)))
#+end_src

The =org-agenda-files= line lives in =:config= because
=org-gtd-directory= is not bound until org-gtd has loaded.

** Recipe: Doom Emacs

Add to =packages.el=:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(package! org-gtd)
#+end_src

Then in =config.el=:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package! org-gtd
  :after org
  :init
  (setq org-gtd-update-ack "4.0.0")
  :custom
  (org-todo-keywords '((sequence "TODO" "NEXT" "WAIT" "|" "DONE" "CNCL")))
  (org-gtd-keyword-mapping '((todo . "TODO") (next . "NEXT")
                             (wait . "WAIT") (done . "DONE")
                             (canceled . "CNCL")))
  :config
  (org-edna-mode)
  (setq org-agenda-files (list org-gtd-directory))
  (map! :leader
        (:prefix ("d" . "org-gtd")
         :desc "Capture"        "c"  #'org-gtd-capture
         :desc "Engage"         "e"  #'org-gtd-engage
         :desc "Process inbox"  "p"  #'org-gtd-process-inbox))
  (map! :map org-gtd-clarify-mode-map
        :desc "Organize this item" "C-c c" #'org-gtd-organize))
#+end_src

After editing, run =doom sync= and restart.

** Recipe: Spacemacs

In =dotspacemacs/user-config=:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package org-gtd
  :ensure t
  :after org
  :init
  (setq org-gtd-update-ack "4.0.0")
  (setq org-todo-keywords
        '((sequence "TODO" "NEXT" "WAIT" "|" "DONE" "CNCL")))
  (setq org-gtd-keyword-mapping
        '((todo . "TODO") (next . "NEXT") (wait . "WAIT")
          (done . "DONE") (canceled . "CNCL")))
  :config
  (org-edna-mode 1)
  (setq org-agenda-files (list org-gtd-directory))
  (spacemacs/set-leader-keys
    "od c" 'org-gtd-capture
    "od e" 'org-gtd-engage
    "od p" 'org-gtd-process-inbox))
(with-eval-after-load 'org-gtd
  (define-key org-gtd-clarify-mode-map (kbd "C-c c") 'org-gtd-organize))
#+end_src

** Confirm it is working

After restarting Emacs:

1. Run =M-x org-gtd-capture= (or your binding, =C-c d c= in the
   recipes above). A capture buffer opens for the inbox. Type a
   short heading, then =C-c C-c= to save.
2. Open =~/gtd/inbox.org=. Your captured heading should be there
   with an =ORG_GTD_CAPTURED_AT= property.
3. Run =M-x org-gtd-process-inbox=. A clarify buffer opens with the
   item you just captured.

If any of those steps fail, see [[#diagnose-and-repair][How to diagnose and repair]].
