* How this journal works
:PROPERTIES:
:VISIBILITY: all
:END:

- *Source of truth*: =docs/feature-analysis/gaps/recommended-not-implemented.md=
  (122 recommended features; 49 implemented; 73 gaps). Per-feature UX designs,
  where they exist, live in =docs/feature-analysis/ux-workflows/REC-*.md=.
- *Process*: pick the next item from [[* Backlog — awaiting decision][Backlog]],
  discuss it, then file a full heading under [[* Decisions][Decisions]] in its
  GTD area with a decision keyword, and check it off the backlog.
- *Decision keywords* (the =#+TODO= line):
  - =UNDECIDED= — not yet reviewed.
  - =REVIEWING= — actively under discussion.
  - =BUILD-V5= — commit to building it for 5.0.0.
  - =DEFER= — real, but later (5.x or a future adapter layer); say where.
  - =DROP= — decided not to do; say why.
  - =DONE= — already implemented.
- *Per-heading properties*: =:REC_ID:=, =:STATUS:= (current implementation
  status: Implemented / Partial / Via-config / Not-implemented), =:STRENGTH:=
  (must / should / may), =:TYPE:= (tool / methodology), =:DISPOSITION:= (prior
  =V-xx= adjudication, if any — those are already-made calls, not to
  re-litigate), =:UX_DOC:= (whether a REC-* workflow design exists).
- *Tags*: strength + area, for filtering (e.g. =C-c \ +must+engage=).
- *Standing constraints* (do not propose against these): no org-ql /
  org-super-agenda dependency; no forced org-agenda; reference and contacts
  systems are future *adapter* layers, not bundled stores.
