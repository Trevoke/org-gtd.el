Repro: open org-gtd-view-manager, open builder (c); if the FIRST action is filtering (e.g. area-of-focus), the transient panel vanishes when the agenda refreshes. Clicking on the agenda window makes the transient reappear.

Cause: org-gtd-view-show -> org-agenda sets up its own windows during the preview render, stealing the layout the transient was drawn in, so the transient loses its window until a command redraws it.

Fix direction: render the preview without disturbing the transient -- e.g. let-bind org-agenda-window-setup to a non-disruptive value (current-window / other-window) or show the agenda in a dedicated side window, and ensure the transient stays visible across preview refreshes.
